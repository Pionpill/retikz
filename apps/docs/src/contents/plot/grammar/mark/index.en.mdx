---
title: Marks
description: Marks decide which shapes a datum or data group creates; field encodings, scales, and coordinates decide where those shapes finally land.
---

Marks are the geometry layer of the graphic grammar. They declare which shape represents the data, without hard-coding pixel positions; visual channels such as position, color, and size are driven by fields, then interpreted by scales and coordinates.

## Mark Types

  <LinkedCard href="/plot/grammar/mark/bar"><strong>Bar</strong><span>Extend from a baseline to a value, including grouped, stacked, and radial bars.</span></LinkedCard>
