---
title: Channels
description: 'Channels bind fields or constants to visual roles such as position, color, and size, giving coordinates, marks, and guides a shared value source.'
---

A channel describes two things: where a value comes from and which visual role it belongs to. For example, `x="gdp"` reads the `gdp` field for position, while `color="region"` reads the `region` field for color. Data owns field preparation; Core and the renderer still own the final drawing semantics and output.

Mark props in the React DSL become JSON-safe field or constant bindings in PlotSpec. At runtime, coordinate-definition roles read position bindings; other visual channels look up a matching definition in the channel registry and resolve into mark properties or guide descriptors.

<ComponentPreview files="channel-flow" size="lg" hideCode />

PlotSpec stores bindings, not functions. Position roles follow the coordinate definition; other built-in and custom definitions enter the same registry, then meet bindings at the resolver. Extending a visual channel therefore does not require a parallel IR or consumption path.

## Responsibilities

| Topic             | Responsibility                                                           | Reach for it when                                                        |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Channel binding   | Explain how fields or constants enter mark props and PlotSpec            | You want to confirm what `x="date"`, `color="region"`, or `y={80}` means |
| Built-in channels | List available channels, default scales, and destinations by visual task | You want to choose channels such as `color`, `size`, or `strokeWidth`    |

To add a project-specific visual channel, continue to [Custom Channels](/viz/plot/extend/channel). Custom capabilities enter through `channelDefinitions` and share the same registry, resolution, and delivery paths as built-ins.
