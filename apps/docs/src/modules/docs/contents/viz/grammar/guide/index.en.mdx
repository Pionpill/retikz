---
title: Guides
description: Guides explain scale and coordinate results to readers, such as axes, grids, and legends; they help read the plot but are not data marks themselves.
---

Guides are the explanatory layer of the graphic grammar. Marks draw the data; guides tell readers what positions, colors, sizes, or shapes mean.
